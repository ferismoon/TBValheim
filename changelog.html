26 September 2026 · Test update

A smoother journey, a fairer fight.

This update is prepared for server testing. Use the pack announced in Discord; these notes do not mean the server has already switched.

New additions

  • Stamina Extended brings together stamina, swimming, fishing and terrain travel adjustments. Fishing pull stamina is set to half its usual cost.
  • Simple Compass adds direction and map-pin guidance.
  • Jam and HoneyPlus add food choices and beekeeping options.
  • Loot Filter lets you choose items to exclude from automatic pickup. Its blacklist starts empty.

Balance and fixes

  • Creature stars: reduced the influence of distance and local kills, removed the extra night boost and made local difficulty decay faster. Biomes and distance still matter, and raids still contribute kills. This aims for a gentler spread; it does not guarantee zero-star spawns everywhere.
  • Raids: doubled the per-player cooldown scaling. Raid checks still use the mod’s per-player system.
  • Backpack Balance: Jude’s Simple Backpack now matches Deer Hide Cape durability and armour; the Heavy Backpack matches Troll Hide Cape. Carry bonuses remain unchanged. Read the guide →
  • Progression Bridge: updated for Star Level System 1.19.0, keeping raid eligibility linked to each character’s boss progress.
  • Adventure Backpacks: updated backpack automation settings, keeping crafting and automatic storage enabled. Crafting uses your main inventory first and leaves one item in a backpack stack.

Repair hotfix

Removed Comfy Auto Repair after reports of incomplete repairs and an unavailable manual repair button. Use the normal repair button at the appropriate station. The latest cleanup script removes it from existing installations.

Backpack Balance 0.1.2 fixes Jude’s backpack stats being reset during world loading. Restart after installing and repair your backpack to fill its new durability.

Replaced mods — cleanup required

Stamina Extended replaces Need for Speed, Reely Good Rod and Vikings Do Swim. Diving and the Atlantean Crown are no longer included. Unequip the crown before updating and run the supplied Remove Old Mods.bat after copying the client pack.

25 September 2026 · Pack update

1.0.1 — A closer view, doors your way

  • Added First Person 1.0.13. Press F6 to toggle the camera view.
  • Added TrueBastards Auto Close Doors 0.2.1 for player-built doors and gates, including wooden gates.
  • Each builder can choose a custom timer, the server default or NeverClose in F1. Personal settings do not require admin rights and are saved by the server.
  • The included door mod fixes the preference-handshake issue in the earlier test build. Dungeon doors and generated-building doors remain excluded.

24 September 2026 · Initial release

1.0 — Ready to set sail

  • Added Epic Loot, Valheim Armory and Jude’s Equipment for more loot and equipment choices.
  • Added Star Level System for creature stars, modifiers and raids.
  • Introduced individual boss progression through World Advancement Progression, with the pack’s progression bridge included.
  • Added building, farming, repair, storage and travel conveniences, including backpacks and a hip lantern.
  • Kept vanilla building support rules and removed ZenDistributor’s cart weight assistance.
  • Kept trader repairs available and set the hip lantern controls to K for light and Alt + K for heat.
  • Tuned the troll raid down: fewer trolls, no extra stars or modifiers, and an Elder progression requirement.
