Default Keybindings

The keyboard and mouse shortcuts supplied with the September 26 test pack. Your own remaps may differ. Most everyday Valheim controls stay in the game’s Settings → Controls menu.

Everyday shortcuts

KeyWhat it does
F1Open mod settings, including your personal door timer.
KToggle your equipped hip lantern’s light.
Left Alt + KToggle hip lantern heat.
IOpen your Adventure Backpack.
LToggle the Adventure Backpack wisplight, when available.
F6Toggle first-person view.
G / H / JUse Epic Loot ability slots 1, 2 and 3. Requires an equipped item granting an ability.

Adventure Backpacks also has Y assigned to quick-drop, but the required Outward Mode is disabled in the pack.

Building and organising

KeyWhen to use it
BEquip or put away your best hammer from your inventory. Hold to select repair.
Left Shift + ← / →With a hammer equipped, cycle building rotation angles.
Left AltHold while interacting with a double door to open or close only that door.
QUse the alternate action shown by supported Zen interactions. Follow the on-screen prompt.
Left Ctrl + left clickWith your hammer equipped, point at furniture, hold Ctrl and click to pick it up. Right-click cancels. Follow the placement prompts.
Middle mouseZenSign’s item search from the inventory.
Left ShiftToggle camera focus on a ZenSign search result.

Move or resize the Daywheel

Put your hammer, hoe or cultivator away, then press F8 to enter the Daywheel’s move mode.

ControlIn move mode
Left mouse dragMove the display.
Arrow keysNudge its position. Shift makes larger steps; Ctrl makes smaller steps.
Page Up / Page DownIncrease or decrease its size.
Home / EndPrevious or next theme.
F8Finish adjusting the display.

Make the controls yours

Open F1 and find the relevant mod to change its available personal bindings. Server-controlled options may be locked. Use the game’s own controls menu for vanilla actions.

If one key triggers two things, rebind one of them. Epic Loot’s G ability is worth checking against your existing game controls. These are the supplied defaults, not a guarantee that every personal setup is conflict-free.
