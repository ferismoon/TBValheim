True Bastards mods · Version 0.1.2

Backpack Balance

A sturdier journey for Jude’s backpacks. Their durability and armour now follow familiar cape tiers, while keeping their carrying bonuses.

BackpackMatches
Simple BackpackDeer Hide Cape durability and armour, including upgrades.
Heavy BackpackTroll Hide Cape durability and armour, including upgrades.

Already own one?

The change applies automatically when the pack loads. Repair your backpack at a suitable station to fill its increased durability.

What stays familiar

Your backpack keeps its carrying bonus, weight, movement penalty and crafting recipe. The patch follows the matching cape’s stats; it does not add a new item or ask you to replace your existing backpack.

Which backpacks?

This covers the Simple Backpack and Heavy Backpack from Jude’s Equipment. Adventure Backpacks is a separate mod with its own bags and storage features.

No setup needed

Included in pack 1.0.2. Install the complete pack on your client; the server uses the matching version. There are no personal settings to adjust.
