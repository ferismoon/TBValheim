True Bastards mods · Version 0.1.1

Progression Bridge

Connects Star Level System’s raid checks to the personal boss progression tracked by World Advancement Progression.

Get the pack on Discord ↗All mods

Progress belongs to your character.

Our pack tracks boss progress individually. The bridge lets Star Level System use the targeted character’s progression when checking which raids are eligible, including on a dedicated server where global boss keys are blocked.

What it does

  • Uses the targeted character’s World Advancement Progression keys alongside world keys for Star Level System’s raid requirements.
  • Waits for a character’s progression data before deciding raid eligibility.
  • Includes personal WAP keys in Star Level System’s local personal-key queries.

Raid chances, biome requirements and cooldowns still apply. The bridge does not force raids to start.

A shared base is still shared.

Eligibility can be personal, but spawned enemies exist in the world. Nearby players and shared buildings can still be caught up in someone else’s raid.

No player setup needed

The bridge comes with the pack and should stay installed on both clients and the server. There are no personal settings to change.

This version is designed for Star Level System 1.19.0 and World Advancement Progression 1.0.0. Use the complete pack instead of independently updating those mods.

What it leaves alone

It does not grant boss kills, alter loot rewards, change creature statistics or adjust star chances. Raid configuration and your saved progression remain managed by their respective mods.

Already defeated a boss before joining?

The bridge does not convert earlier boss kills into WAP’s personal progress. If gear is blocked with “The Gods Reject You,” contact us in Discord and explain which bosses that character has defeated. Restoring earlier progress is separate from this bridge.
