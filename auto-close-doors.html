True Bastards mods · Version 0.2.1

Auto Close Doors

Leave the door duty to the timer. Player-built doors and gates close automatically, with a preference chosen by the person who built them.

Get the pack on Discord ↗All mods

Your build, your choice.

Choose a custom timer, follow the server default, or keep your doors open. Ordinary players can change their own settings.

Gates included.

Wooden gates and other player-built gates using normal door behaviour are included. Crypt doors and doors in generated buildings are left alone.

Remembered for you.

Your choice is saved per character and world on the server. It still applies while you are offline, whoever opens your door.

Choose your timer

  1. Join the server and allow your preferences to synchronize.
  2. Open F1 → TrueBastards Auto Close Doors → 2 - My doors.
  3. Choose a mode below. For a custom timer, select CustomTimer and set Custom delay seconds.
ModeWhat happens
ServerDefaultYour doors follow the server’s current default.
CustomTimerYour doors close after your chosen delay, from 1 to 3600 seconds.
NeverCloseYour doors remain open until someone closes them manually.

Changing only the number while still in ServerDefault mode does not activate your personal timer.

For example

Beanie chooses NeverClose and SettDravan chooses 30 seconds. Beanie’s doors stay open; SettDravan’s doors close after 30 seconds—even when Beanie is the one opening them.

How doors behave

  • Existing player-built doors work when they have a recorded builder.
  • Closing and reopening starts a new countdown.
  • Paired doors each follow their own builder’s preference.
  • Changing a timer applies to doors already open. A shorter delay may close an overdue door promptly.
  • Doors requiring keys or which cannot normally close are excluded.
  • Unloaded areas are not kept running. An overdue door can close when its area loads again.

Server controls

Admins use 1 - Server to enable or disable automatic closing globally and change the default delay.

The mod ships with a 60-second default, but the server can change it. A default of zero leaves ServerDefault doors open; a player’s custom timer can still apply while the feature is enabled.

Installation and updates

Included with the pack. Use the pack installation instructions; no separate download is needed. The same door-mod version must be installed on the server and every client. The pack includes its BepInEx and Jötunn dependencies.

If a door stays open

  • Check that the door was built by your character and that your mode is CustomTimer if you set a personal delay.
  • Check you installed the current pack/update and restarted Valheim.
  • Wait for your preferences to synchronize after joining. Settings made in the main menu are not submitted.
  • Admins may have disabled the feature globally.

If it still does not close, tell us in Discord which door, who built it and which mode you selected. Include your client log if possible.
